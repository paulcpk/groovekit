# Jungle

Fast, chopped breakbeats over deep, rolling sub-bass, with reggae and dub in its roots: rewired drum breaks, booming subs, dub sirens and soulful pads. Chaotic drums, heavy and calm low end.

## The feeling

Energetic on top, deep and slow underneath: the breaks run at 170 while the bass moves at half speed like dub. Raw, rough-edged, urban, with moments of soul and warmth. The drum edits are the art.

## The parts

- **Drums (breaks)**: the core: classic funk breaks chopped and resequenced into new patterns, pitched up, with rolls and edits. One main break, maybe a second for variation. Words: "amen break", "chopped funk breakbeat", "jungle drum break".
- **Kick**: often within the break; an extra kick may reinforce it. Words: "punchy breakbeat kick".
- **Bass**: deep, round sub-bass with slow, reggae-like lines; sometimes a growling reese. As heavy as the drums. Words: "deep jungle sub bass", "reggae sub bassline", "dark reese bass".
- **Snare**: the break's snare; an extra snare layer can add crack. Words: "cracking breakbeat snare".
- **Hats and percussion**: mostly from the break; extra ride or shaker sparingly.
- **Music**: soulful pads, jazzy chords, dub stabs with delay, ragga or reggae samples. Words: "dub stab with delay", "soulful jungle pad", "jazzy chord loop".
- **Vocal**: ragga MC samples, soul vocal snippets. Words: "ragga vocal sample", "soul vocal snippet".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: dub sirens, reverb throws, rewind sounds. Words: "dub siren", "rewind effect".

## Build it in this order

1. The break, chopped and rearranged into a groove you like.
2. The sub-bass, slow and deep against the fast drums.
3. A pad or dub stab for mood; vocal samples.
4. Edits: rolls, stops, rewinds at phrase ends.

## Mix

- Breaks loud and crunchy, high-passed so the sub has the lows alone.
- Sub-bass clean and heavy; mono.
- Not too loud: −9 to −7 LUFS, the breaks need their transients.

## Arrangement

- Intro with pads or a vocal, the break and bass drop together, 16 and 32-bar phrases with edits, a breakdown, a second drop.

## Don't

- No four-on-the-floor kicks.
- No more than two breaks at once.
- No muddy bass: the sub is clean, the drums carry the grit.
