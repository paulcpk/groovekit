# Drum and bass

Fast, tight and powerful: a two-step beat at 174 with a snare on 2 and 4 that cracks, a big, moving bass (sub or reese), and either dark and techy or rolling and melodic atmospheres. Precision and weight.

## The feeling

Driving and urgent, but with a big, slow low end underneath. Rollers glide; dark, techy tunes growl; liquid tunes float on chords and vocals. The drums are tight and clean, the bass is a living thing.

## The parts

- **Kick**: punchy and tight, on 1 and the "and" of 2 or similar two-step patterns. Words: "punchy dnb kick", "tight kick".
- **Snare**: on 2 and 4, loud, cracking, layered; the loudest drum. Words: "dnb snare", "cracking layered snare".
- **Hats and ride**: fast, crisp 16ths, shakers or a ride for drive; or a top break loop layered quietly. Words: "crisp dnb hats", "fast shaker", "top loop break".
- **Drums (loops)**: an optional break loop high-passed under the main beat. Words: "rolling drum break".
- **Bass**: the second star: a clean sub (liquid, rollers) or a moving, filtered reese or growl (neuro, dark). Words: "rolling reese bass", "deep dnb sub", "dark growl bass".
- **Music**: liquid: pads, pianos, strings, jazzy chords; dark: atmospheric drones and stabs. Words: "liquid dnb pad", "atmospheric dark drone", "jazzy dnb chords".
- **Vocal**: soulful lines for liquid, MC phrases or chopped vocals otherwise. Words: "liquid vocal", "dnb mc".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: risers, impacts, sweeps at transitions.

## Build it in this order

1. Kick and snare in a two-step pattern; tight and punchy.
2. The bass: the main hook of the drop.
3. Hats, ride or a quiet top break.
4. Atmosphere and music; intro and breakdown.

## Mix

- Snare loud and bright, kick tight; the sub clean and mono under them, the mid-bass ducked under the drums.
- Very controlled highs: crisp but not sharp.
- Loud: −7 to −5 LUFS.

## Arrangement

- A long intro (32 bars) for DJs, a build, the drop (32 bars), a breakdown, a second drop, outro.

## Don't

- Don't let the bass and the kick fight; don't stack more than two bass layers.
- No loose, sloppy drums: precision is part of the sound.
- No muddy low mids.
