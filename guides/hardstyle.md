# Hardstyle

Epic and euphoric: a distorted, pitched kick that is also the bassline, huge melodies and leads, and dramatic breakdowns that build to massive drops.

## The feeling

Larger than life: euphoric melodies and a punishing kick. The breakdowns are cinematic and emotional, the drops are a wall of energy. Big contrasts: calm and epic, then hard and pounding.

## The parts

- **Kick**: the signature: a punchy attack, then a long, distorted, pitched tail (the "reverse bass" plays on the off-beats, or the kick's tail follows the melody's root notes). Words: "hardstyle kick", "distorted pitched kick", "reverse bass kick".
- **Bass**: the kick is the bass in the drops; in breakdowns a sub or a soft bass under the chords. Words: "sub bass", "soft synth bass".
- **Hats**: small and off-beat, they barely matter. Words: "tight offbeat hat".
- **Clap or snare**: a hard clap on 2 and 4 in the drops; snare rolls in builds. Words: "hardstyle clap", "snare roll build".
- **Percussion**: little; occasional toms in builds.
- **Music**: big supersaw leads, screeches, epic chords and pianos in the breakdowns; the melody is the hook. Words: "euphoric supersaw lead", "hardstyle screech", "epic piano chords", "cinematic pad".
- **Vocal**: heroic or emotional vocal lines, often in the breakdown, or shouted MC phrases. Words: "epic vocal", "MC shout".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: risers, impacts, downlifters, cinematic hits. Words: "big riser", "cinematic impact".

## Build it in this order

1. The melody and chords: hardstyle lives or dies by the hook.
2. The kick, pitched and tuned to the melody's root notes.
3. Clap and hats for the drop.
4. Breakdown: piano or pads with the melody, vocal, build with snare rolls and risers.

## Mix

- In the drops, the kick and the lead are equally loud; nothing else competes.
- Sidechain the leads and pads hard to the kick.
- Very loud master (−5 to −3 LUFS), clipping the kick into a limiter is part of the sound.

## Arrangement

- Intro, a first drop (kick and short melody), a long emotional breakdown, a climactic build, the main drop with the full lead, outro.
- Breakdowns of 16 to 32 bars without the kick.

## Don't

- No subtle, minimal grooves: it needs a big hook.
- No busy hats or percussion: they only clutter the kick.
- Don't let the kick and lead fight: duck the lead under each kick.
