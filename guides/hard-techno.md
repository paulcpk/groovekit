# Hard techno

Fast, distorted and driving: a pounding, saturated kick that is half bass, rave stabs and screaming synths over a relentless groove. Raw energy, but still built on a clear, simple rhythm.

## The feeling

Intense and euphoric in a dark way: pounding, fast, a little dangerous. The kick hits you in the chest every beat; the stabs and leads lift the room. Raw and loud, but every element has its place, so it stays powerful instead of noisy.

## The parts

- **Kick**: the main event: hard, distorted, with a long, saturated tail that carries the bass notes (often tuned to the key). Words: "hard distorted techno kick", "saturated rumble kick", "punchy hard kick with tail".
- **Bass**: often none, since the kick tail is the bass; or a short distorted off-beat bass between kicks. Never fight the kick. Words: "distorted offbeat bass", "gritty rolling bass".
- **Hats**: tight, fast off-beat hats or 16ths, slightly distorted, kept small. Words: "tight distorted hat", "fast techno hi hat".
- **Clap or snare**: a hard clap on 2 and 4 or only in builds; snare rolls in builds. Words: "hard rave clap", "snare roll".
- **Percussion**: one driving loop, distorted toms or metal. Words: "distorted percussion loop", "industrial tom hits".
- **Music**: rave stabs, hoover or acid lines, screaming leads in the drops; short, hooky and repetitive. Words: "rave stab", "hoover synth", "acid screech", "dark hard lead".
- **Vocal**: short shouted or chopped rave vocals. Words: "rave vocal shout", "chopped hard vocal".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: risers and impacts for builds and drops. Words: "noise riser", "impact hit".

## Build it in this order

1. The kick, tuned, distorted and with its tail: it should already move the room on its own.
2. Hats and one percussion loop.
3. The main stab or lead hook.
4. Clap, build-ups (snare rolls, risers), drop.

## Mix

- The kick owns everything below 150 Hz and is the loudest thing; duck anything else with low end.
- Distortion makes highs harsh fast: high-shelf down the leads and hats, keep fizz under control.
- Very loud masters (−6 to −4 LUFS), but the kick has to stay punchy, not flattened.

## Arrangement

- 5 to 6 minutes. Intro of kick and hats, a build into the first drop with the hook, a breakdown with the lead or vocal and a riser, a second, bigger drop, a stripped outro.
- Builds over 8 or 16 bars: snare rolls, rising filters, risers.

## Don't

- Don't stack a separate bass under the kick tail.
- No more than one percussion loop and one lead at a time.
- No constant white-noise wash; risers only in builds.
- Don't let distortion turn everything into fizz.
