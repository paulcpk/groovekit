# Juke and footwork

Fast and stuttering, from Chicago's dance battles: 808 kicks in syncopated, triplet patterns at 160 BPM, rapid hats, and short chopped vocal and soul samples looping obsessively. Feels both twice as fast and half-time.

## The feeling

Restless and playful: the rhythm keeps tripping over itself, the vocal loops repeat until they become hypnotic. Raw, minimal, made for footwork dancers; the space between the hits matters as much as the hits.

## The parts

- **Kick**: an 808 kick, booming and long, in syncopated triplet-based patterns (often grouped 3-3-2 over the bar). Words: "808 kick", "booming 808".
- **Bass**: usually the 808 kick itself; sometimes a sub following the kicks.
- **Snare or clap**: claps and snares on 2 and 4 at half-time feel, or in rolls. Words: "808 clap", "short snare".
- **Hats**: fast 16th or triplet hats, sometimes rolling. Words: "808 hi hat", "fast closed hat".
- **Percussion**: toms, rims, cowbells in triplets: they make the polyrhythm. Words: "808 tom", "808 rim", "808 cowbell".
- **Music**: a short soul, R&B or jazz sample, chopped and looped; or eerie synth pads. Words: "soul sample chop", "rnb loop", "eerie synth pad".
- **Vocal**: the hook: a tiny vocal fragment repeated and stuttered. Words: "chopped vocal stutter", "short soul vocal".

## Build it in this order

1. The vocal or sample chop, repeating.
2. The 808 kick pattern in triplets against it.
3. Claps and hats; toms for polyrhythm.
4. Variation by muting parts and changing the chop.

## Mix

- Booming 808s, raw and loud; little processing, a gritty, unpolished feel.
- Keep the vocal chop clear and present.

## Arrangement

- Short tracks (3 to 4 minutes), loops of 2 or 4 bars changing by subtraction and addition.

## Don't

- No four-on-the-floor; no smooth, polished production.
- Don't fill every 16th: the gaps make the rhythm.
