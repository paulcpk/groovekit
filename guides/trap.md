# Trap

Dark, heavy and half-time: a booming 808 that slides, hard snares or claps on the 3rd beat, rapid hi-hat rolls, and sparse, moody melodies. Space and weight.

## The feeling

Menacing and confident. The 808 shakes the room, the hats tick nervously, the melody is dark and simple. Half-time: it feels slow even at 140 BPM. Lots of empty space between the hits.

## The parts

- **Kick**: short and punchy, often layered with the 808's start. Words: "trap kick", "punchy short kick".
- **Bass (808)**: the star: a long, tuned 808 with some distortion, gliding between notes, following the melody's roots. As loud as or louder than the kick. Words: "distorted 808", "long trap 808", "gliding 808 bass".
- **Snare or clap**: on the 3rd beat of each half-time bar (beats 2 and 4 at half speed), hard and bright. Words: "trap snare", "hard trap clap".
- **Hats**: the signature: 8ths and 16ths with rolls and triplets (32nds, 1/16 triplets), pitched up and down. Quiet but constant. Words: "trap hi hat", "crisp closed hat".
- **Percussion**: sparse rims, snaps, open hats on off-beats.
- **Music**: a dark, simple melody: bells, plucks, pianos, flutes, choirs, strings, minor or phrygian. Words: "dark trap bell melody", "eerie piano", "dark flute loop", "choir pad".
- **Vocal**: ad-libs, chopped vocal phrases. Words: "vocal ad lib", "dark vocal chop".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: reverse cymbals, risers before the drop.

## Build it in this order

1. The melody: dark, short, repeating.
2. The 808 following the melody's roots, with slides.
3. Kick, then snare on the 3rd beat.
4. Hats with rolls; then variations every 4 or 8 bars.

## Mix

- The 808 and kick own the lows; never two sustained bass layers. Sidechain or carve so the kick punches through the 808.
- Hats quiet and crisp; the melody under the vocal range.
- Loud: −8 to −6 LUFS.

## Arrangement

- Intro with the melody alone, 16-bar hooks and verses, drops where the 808 and drums return together, short drum stops before the hook.

## Don't

- No four-on-the-floor; no straight house hats.
- No busy melodies or many layers: space is the sound.
- Don't let the 808 and the kick muddy each other.
