# Schranz

Relentless, loop-driven and percussive: a hammering kick under dense, distorted, repeating drum loops that lock into a hypnotic machine groove. Almost no melody; the drums are the music.

## The feeling

A machine running at full speed: hypnotic, pounding, physical. The loops grind against each other and the kick hammers through. Raw and repetitive on purpose; the tension comes from filtering and swapping loops, not from melodies.

## The parts

- **Kick**: short, hard and punchy, often slightly distorted, every beat. Shorter than a hard-techno kick: the loops fill the space. Words: "short hard kick", "pounding distorted kick".
- **Bass**: little or none; a low, distorted rumble at most. Words: "distorted low rumble".
- **Drum loops (drums)**: the core: chopped, distorted, filtered percussion loops, often with a rolling, galloping pattern. Two loops at most, one low-mid and one higher. Words: "distorted schranz loop", "grinding percussion loop", "filtered industrial drum loop".
- **Hats**: driving, slightly distorted hats or rides. Words: "distorted ride", "driving metallic hat".
- **Clap or snare**: rare, hard and short. Words: "short hard clap".
- **Percussion**: metallic hits, distorted toms. Words: "metallic industrial hit", "distorted tom".
- **Music**: a single short, noisy stab or a stuttering vocal at most. Words: "noisy industrial stab".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: filter sweeps on the loops themselves do most of the work. Words: "industrial noise sweep".

## Build it in this order

1. Kick and the main drum loop, locked together.
2. A second, contrasting loop (higher or lower).
3. Hats or ride to drive.
4. Filter movement (automate the loops' low-pass and high-pass) and occasional hits.

## Mix

- The kick stays on top; the loops are loud but under it. High-pass the loops so the low end stays clean.
- Distortion is part of the sound, but watch the 2 to 5 kHz harshness.
- Very loud master, around −6 to −4 LUFS.

## Arrangement

- Long, DJ-friendly blocks of 16 and 32 bars; changes by swapping or filtering loops.
- Short breaks (a few bars without the kick), never long breakdowns.

## Don't

- No melodic leads or chord progressions.
- No more than two loops at once.
- No clean, polite drums: grit is the point, but keep the kick clear of it.
