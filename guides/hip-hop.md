# Hip-hop

A head-nodding beat: a hard kick and a cracking snare with swing, a sample or loop with character (soul, jazz, funk), a deep bass, and space for a voice. Groove and attitude over complexity.

## The feeling

Laid back but heavy: you nod your head on every snare. Dusty, warm samples with soul; drums that knock; a little lazy, a little behind the beat. The beat leaves room for someone to rap over it.

## The parts

- **Kick**: punchy and knocking, often a bit dusty; patterns are syncopated, not four on the floor. Words: "boom bap kick", "dusty punchy kick", "knocking hip hop kick".
- **Bass**: a deep sub or a warm bass that follows the sample's root notes; sometimes just the kick. Words: "deep hip hop bass", "warm sub bass", "jazzy upright bass".
- **Snare**: the backbone on 2 and 4: cracking, loud, as loud as the kick. Words: "boom bap snare", "dusty cracking snare", "fat hip hop snare".
- **Hats**: swung 8ths or 16ths, quiet, a little loose. Words: "swung dusty hat", "lo fi hi hat".
- **Percussion**: sparse: a shaker, a rim, a tambourine.
- **Drums (loops)**: a sampled drum break can replace the single hits. Words: "dusty drum break", "funk breakbeat".
- **Music**: the sample: a soul, jazz or funk loop, chopped and maybe pitched; or Rhodes, piano, strings. Words: "dusty soul sample", "jazzy rhodes loop", "vinyl soul chop", "lo fi piano".
- **Vocal**: chopped vocal samples, scratches, short phrases. Words: "soul vocal chop", "vinyl scratch".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: vinyl crackle, very low.

## Build it in this order

1. The sample or chords: they set the mood.
2. Kick and snare with swing, sitting behind the beat.
3. Hats, quiet and swung; bass.
4. Small changes: drop the drums for a bar, filter the sample, add a chop.

## Mix

- Kick and snare loud and punchy; the snare as loud as the kick.
- The sample warm and a bit filtered, so the drums cut through. Leave the mids open for a voice.
- Not too loud: −10 to −8 LUFS, keep the drums' knock.

## Arrangement

- Loops of 4 or 8 bars; intro, verses of 16 bars, hooks of 8, drops where the drums stop for a moment.

## Don't

- No four-on-the-floor kicks, no quantised, robotic drums.
- No busy hats or trap rolls (that's trap).
- No cluttered mids: leave room for the voice.
