# House

Warm, groovy and uplifting: a four-on-the-floor kick, a bouncing bassline, off-beat open hats, a clap on 2 and 4, and chords or vocals with soul. It swings and makes you smile.

## The feeling

Joyful, warm and human. The groove bounces; the chords and vocals give it soul. It invites everyone onto the floor rather than overwhelming them. Funk, disco and gospel are in its blood.

## The parts

- **Kick**: punchy and round, not too long. Words: "punchy house kick", "warm round kick".
- **Bass**: bouncy and melodic, often off-beat or syncopated, a warm sub or a plucky bass. Words: "groovy house bassline", "warm plucky bass", "deep sub bass".
- **Hats**: the off-beat open hat is the signature, plus swung closed 16ths or a shaker. Words: "offbeat open hat", "swung closed hat", "shaker loop".
- **Clap or snare**: a clap on 2 and 4, bright but warm. Words: "house clap", "warm snappy clap".
- **Percussion**: congas, bongos, tambourine, with swing. Words: "latin conga loop", "tambourine", "bongo groove".
- **Music**: organ, piano or Rhodes chords (minor 7ths, 9ths), stabs, filtered disco loops, a simple hook. Words: "house piano chords", "deep house rhodes", "organ stab", "filtered disco loop".
- **Vocal**: soulful vocal phrases or chops; the hook. Words: "soulful house vocal", "diva vocal chop", "gospel vocal".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: filter sweeps, short risers, crowd noise at most.

## Build it in this order

1. Kick, bass and chords: the groove and the harmony.
2. Clap, off-beat open hat, swung closed hats or shaker.
3. Percussion for bounce.
4. Vocal hook, then arrange.

## Mix

- Kick and bass share the low end: duck the bass a little under each kick.
- Hats and percussion bright but not sharp; chords warm, vocals up front.
- Around −9 to −7 LUFS, with groove and dynamics left in.

## Arrangement

- 5 to 7 minutes. Intro with drums for DJs, bass and chords enter, a breakdown with the vocal or chords alone, a filter build, the full groove back, outro.
- Changes every 8 or 16 bars.

## Don't

- No stiff, unswung hats.
- No dark, industrial sounds or distorted kicks.
- Don't leave the bass static: it should move.
- Don't overload the top with several hat and shaker loops.
