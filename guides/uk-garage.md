# UK garage

Shuffling, skippy and soulful: a swung 2-step beat with the kick skipping beats, crisp shuffled hats, a rolling bassline, and chopped, pitched vocals. Bouncy, sexy and light on its feet.

## The feeling

Swinging and smooth, a little cheeky. The beat skips rather than marches; the vocals are pitched and chopped into hooks; the bass rolls under it. Late-night, warm, made for moving your hips.

## The parts

- **Kick**: punchy and short, in 2-step patterns: not on every beat, skipping and syncopated. Words: "uk garage kick", "punchy short kick".
- **Bass**: a rolling, warm bassline: sub, organ bass or a slightly growling reese. Words: "garage bassline", "rolling sub bass", "organ bass".
- **Snare or clap**: crisp snare or clap on 2 and 4, sometimes with ghost notes. Words: "garage snare", "crisp clap".
- **Hats**: heavily swung, shuffling 16ths with open hats on off-beats: the groove lives here. Words: "shuffled garage hats", "swung 2 step hat".
- **Percussion**: rims, shakers, toms that skip with the kicks. Words: "garage rim shot", "swung shaker".
- **Music**: organ stabs, warm chords (minor 7ths and 9ths), plucks, strings. Words: "garage organ stab", "warm rnb chords", "plucky stab".
- **Vocal**: pitched, chopped R&B vocals, often re-sequenced into new rhythms. Words: "pitched rnb vocal chop", "garage vocal".
- **Atmosphere (fx)**: reverse cymbals, short sweeps.

## Build it in this order

1. Kick, snare and swung hats: get the 2-step skip right (swing about 25 to 35%).
2. Bassline rolling with the kicks.
3. Chords or organ stabs; vocal chops.
4. Percussion for extra skip.

## Mix

- Hats crisp and audible: the swing must be heard. Bass warm and round, ducked under the kick.
- Vocals bright and present. Around −9 to −7 LUFS.

## Arrangement

- 4 to 6 minutes; intro with drums, vocal hooks every 16 bars, a breakdown with vocals and chords, the bass drop back in.

## Don't

- No four-on-the-floor kick, no straight hats.
- No dark, heavy industrial sounds.
- Don't stack hats: one shuffled pattern plus an open hat is enough.
